# 📊 Global Sales Performance Dashboard (2011–2014)

## 🔍 Overview

This project presents an interactive **Global Sales Dashboard** analyzing business performance from 2011 to 2014.
The goal is to uncover key trends in **sales, profit, customer behavior, and market performance** to support data-driven decision-making.

## 📈 Business Insights

### 🧑‍💼 Customer & Segment Analysis

* **Consumer segment** drives the business with ~51.5% of total sales
* Corporate and Home Office segments contribute moderate but stable revenue

### 🌍 Market Performance

* **APAC (3.6M) and EU (2.9M)** are the highest revenue-generating markets
* Canada shows minimal contribution, indicating a low-performing region

### 📍 Regional Trends

* Central and South regions dominate in sales
* Several smaller regions show declining or low performance → growth opportunity

### 📦 Product Insights

* Top-performing products generate up to **25K profit**
* Certain products are consistently **loss-making**, impacting overall profitability
